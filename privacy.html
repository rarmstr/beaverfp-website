Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Your Privacy is Our Priority

Your financial data stays yours. Beaver FP has no servers and no accounts. Your plan is stored on your device and, with a subscription, syncs to your other Apple devices through your own iCloud account - we never see it, and we have no way to. The app does send usage analytics, which never include your plan; section 1 says exactly what they contain.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Beaver FP operates on a fundamentally different model than most apps:

  • Your Plan Is Never Collected: We do not collect, store, or transmit your plans, or any personal or financial information you enter. There is no Beaver FP account to create and no back end to send your plan to.
  • Stored on Your Device: Your financial plans, personal information, and calculations are stored on your device.
  • Synced Through Your Own iCloud: With a subscription and an iCloud account, your plan also syncs to your other Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own Apple Account. It is your iCloud storage, under your Apple Account - see section 3.
  • No Beaver FP Servers: We do not operate servers or databases. Your data never passes through infrastructure we control, and we cannot access, view, or recover it.
  • Usage Analytics: To learn which parts of the app are used, the app sends usage events to HeyCatch, Inc., an analytics service in the United States, which stores them with PostHog's US cloud. The events contain:
    • which screens you open, when the app opens and closes, whether you finish Quick Plan or the guided tour, and which subscription you buy and whether it began with the free trial
    • your device's model, operating system, app version, screen size, language, time zone, and whether it is on Wi-Fi or cellular
    • a random ID that the app creates when it is installed, so events from the same device can be counted together
    • your internet (IP) address, which reaches HeyCatch with every request. HeyCatch uses it to look up your approximate city and country.
    The events never contain your plans, any amount, age or name you enter, your email, or your Apple Account. The random ID is not connected to any of those, and we do not try to connect it. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with data brokers. HeyCatch keeps it while Beaver FP uses its service, and for two months after.
  • Website Analytics: beaverfp.com also uses HeyCatch. When you visit, it records the pages you view, how far you scroll, the links and buttons you click, the site or campaign link that sent you, your browser, device type and screen size, and your IP address, from which HeyCatch looks up your approximate city and country. It stores a random ID in a cookie and in your browser's local storage for up to a year, so a later visit from the same browser counts as the same visitor. It does not record what you type, and it is not used for advertising. Deleting this site's cookies and data in your browser removes the ID.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: We do not use advertising, and we do not link app or website data with data from other companies' apps or websites.

2. How Your Data is Protected

On-Device Architecture

Your data is stored using Apple's secure storage systems on your device, and synced only through Apple's iCloud. This architecture protects your privacy because:

  • Nothing for Us to Breach: There is no Beaver FP database to attack, because we hold no copy of your plan. We cannot lose what we never have.
  • No Third Parties: Your plan travels between your own devices and your own iCloud account, and nowhere else. It is never sold, shared, or handed to an advertiser, data broker, or analytics provider.
  • Complete Control: You decide whether to sync at all, and when to delete or export your data.
  • Works Offline: No internet connection is required to build or view your financial plans. If you are offline, your device catches up the next time it connects.

Apple Security Features

Your data benefits from Apple's industry-leading security features:

  • App Sandbox: Your financial data is isolated from other apps. Each app runs in its own secure sandbox, preventing unauthorized access to your Beaver FP data.
  • Data Protection: When your device is locked, your data is automatically encrypted using hardware encryption. Even with physical access to your device, your financial information cannot be read without authentication.
  • Biometric Authentication: Face ID or Touch ID can protect access to the app. Your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave on your device.
  • Secure Enclave: Sensitive operations are processed in a dedicated security coprocessor, providing hardware-level protection.

3. iCloud Sync

Beaver FP keeps your plan up to date across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. Syncing across devices is part of a paid subscription. With a subscription, sync is on by default when you are signed in to iCloud, and you can turn it off. Without one, your plans stay on the device you are using.

  • Your iCloud Account, Not Ours: Your plan is written to the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account. Apple does not give developers access to the private database - we have no console for it, no copy of it, and no way to read it.
  • What Syncs: The financial plans you create - your inputs, overrides and the settings attached to them - so the same plan is available on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Encryption: Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and at rest. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection for your Apple Account, your iCloud data is end-to-end encrypted, so not even Apple can read it.
  • Turning Sync Off: You can turn sync off in Beaver FP's settings. Your plan then stays on the device you are using, and your device stops sending changes to iCloud.
  • If a Subscription Ends: Syncing stops. Nothing is deleted - the plans already on your devices and in your iCloud stay exactly where they are, and syncing resumes if you subscribe again.
  • If You Are Not Signed In: Beaver FP works fully without iCloud. With no iCloud account signed in, the app is local-only and nothing is uploaded.
  • Deleting Synced Data: Deleting a plan in the app removes it from your devices and from iCloud. You can also remove the app's stored iCloud data at any time from your device's Settings, under your name → iCloud.
  • Apple's Role: Apple stores this data as your iCloud provider under Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms. It is not shared with us, and Apple does not act as our service provider - the storage is yours.

4. iCloud Device Backup (Optional)

Separately from sync, if you use iCloud Backup for your device:

  • Your Beaver FP data may be included in your device's iCloud backup.
  • Apple encrypts device backups, and they are end-to-end encrypted if you have Advanced Data Protection turned on.
  • You control iCloud backup settings in your device's Settings app.
  • We do not have access to your iCloud account or backups.

5. Third-Party Services

Beaver FP uses the following services:

  • iCloud / CloudKit: For syncing your plan between your own devices, in your own Apple Account, as described in section 3. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and updates. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • StoreKit (In-App Purchases): For subscription management. Apple processes all payment information. We never see your credit card or payment details.
  • HeyCatch (Usage Analytics): Receives the usage events described in section 1, from the app and from this website, and stores them with PostHog in the United States. Subject to HeyCatch's privacy policy at heycatch.ai/privacy.

We do not use any advertising or tracking services, and we share no data with data brokers.

6. Your Data Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Right to Delete: You can delete any financial plan or all data instantly from within the app, which removes it from your devices and from your iCloud storage.
  • Right to Export: You can export your plans in standard formats for portability.
  • Right to Privacy: We never ask for unnecessary permissions or data.
  • Right to Choose: Sync is a feature, not a requirement. Turn it off and Beaver FP is a local-only app again.
  • Right to Transparency: When the way Beaver FP handles data changes, as it did when iCloud sync arrived in version 2.1 and usage analytics arrived in version 2.6, we update this policy and say what changed. What will not change is that we hold no copy of your plan and have no way to see it.
  • Usage Analytics: The app has no setting to turn usage analytics off; deleting the app stops them. For questions, email support@beaverfp.com. The random ID is not shown in the app and is not connected to you, so we cannot pick out one person's events; we can tell you what the events contain.

7. Children's Privacy

Beaver FP is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The only data the app sends is the usage analytics in section 1, which contain no names, contact details or plan information. Because plans are stored on the device and in the account holder's own iCloud, parents retain complete control over their child's use of the app.

9. International Users

Your plan is never transferred to us. The usage analytics described in section 1 are processed and stored in the United States by HeyCatch and PostHog, so they leave Canada.

If iCloud sync is on, your plan is stored in your own iCloud account on Apple's infrastructure. Apple operates that infrastructure globally, so data associated with your Apple Account may be stored or processed outside your own country, including outside Canada. That storage is governed by your agreement with Apple, not by us. If you would rather your plan never leave your device, turn sync off in Beaver FP's settings.

10. Data Security Best Practices

To maximize your privacy and security when using Beaver FP:

  • Keep your device's operating system up to date
  • Use a strong device passcode
  • Enable Face ID or Touch ID for app access
  • Protect your Apple Account with a strong password and two-factor authentication - with sync on, that account guards your plan
  • Consider turning on Advanced Data Protection for end-to-end encrypted iCloud data
  • Do not share your device with untrusted individuals
  • Regularly back up your device using iCloud or your computer
