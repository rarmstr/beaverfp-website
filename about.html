About Beaver FP

Beaver FP is a retirement planning app for Canadians, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It lays out your retirement year by year — CPP, OAS, GIS, TFSA, RRSP and federal and provincial tax — and shows which changes are worth making, each priced in today's dollars.

Who builds it

Beaver FP is built by Rob Armstrong, an independent developer in St. Albert, Alberta, who has been working on it since 2024. On the App Store it is published under Rob's full name, Robert Armstrong.

“Every feature in this app started as a user suggestion or a personal need I discovered while planning my own retirement.”

What it's built around

  • Retirement planning, and nothing else. No bank logins, no transactions to sort, no budgets to track.
  • Canadian rules. CPP, OAS with clawback, GIS, every registered account, and federal and provincial tax for every province and territory.
  • Your plan stays yours. There are no Beaver FP servers and no accounts. Your plan is stored on your devices and, with a subscription, in your own iCloud.
  • Nothing is hidden. Tap any number for the arithmetic behind it, and override any value in any year.
  • A planning tool, not advice. Beaver FP complements your financial advisor; it doesn't replace one.
